What’s in the box?

Nishant Shukla

2026-08-15

As we’re reading and writing fewer lines of code, there’s a collective feeling of dread. You just know something’s missing. Where’s that sense of click when types match or that pride when the code becomes the documentation?

Coding agents are pretty good at “getting the job done.” But, what if the “job” was never about writing the code, but instead about understanding it. What if engineering an idea into code was itself the outcome to appreciate? Quantum has these properties (for now); you can go wild designing algorithms that nobody today can run. That means you can wallow in code.

So, where do you start? Don’t make my mistake… I started reading papers and they skip over the “fun” parts!

After getting stuck on a JEPA-esque prototype, I went deep in quantum: wrote a few research papers (QCE 2026 accepted, POPL submitted, Quantum drafted), submitted a Unitary Foundation microgrant, and got an opportunity to present a tutorial at IEEE Quantum Week 2026.

For those that can’t make it to Quantum Week this year, I’ll be uploading slides and speaker notes here every week: Introduction to Quantum Oracle Engineering.
